Settings

Edit, share, or Publish to the web link. The deck must be viewable by anyone with the link.

Google caches the deck, so edits need a reload to appear. Reloading returns to slide 1.

Hover any panel on the board for − + ✕ (smaller, bigger, hide), and drag its edge to give it more or less room.

Must allow hot-linking — a Google Drive share link will not play. Falls back to the built-in chime.

Browsers block audio until someone clicks. Press Enable sound once after the display starts each day.

Weather comes from Open-Meteo — free, no account. The quote of the day changes at midnight.

Past dates are ignored, so old entries can stay. Click the countdown on the board to see the one after.

The period names on the board (P1, Break, Lunch…) and the Week A/B calendar are set in the SCHEDULE and WEEKS lists near the top of the page source.
