Settings

Saved in this browser. Changes apply immediately.

Paste the normal edit link, a share link, or a File → Share → Publish to the web link. The deck must be viewable by anyone with the link (or by everyone at your school domain).

Google caches the embedded deck, so edits you make in Slides do not appear by themselves. Reloading picks up the current version — but it jumps back to slide 1, so a longer gap is calmer for a display in a lesson. Refresh now forces it immediately.

Optional. Must be a direct link that allows hot-linking — a Google Drive share link will not play. If it fails to load, the built-in chime plays instead.

Browsers block audio until someone clicks. Press Enable sound once after the display starts up each day.

Shows the days left until the next one. Dates in the past are ignored automatically, so old entries can stay in the list. Click the countdown on the bar to see the one after. Leave empty to hide it.

Weather comes from Open-Meteo — free, no account, no tracking. Needs internet; if it is unreachable the weather panel simply hides itself. The quote of the day changes automatically at midnight.
